# Sunbeam English School, Bhagwanpur — Website

A modern, responsive, and accessible school website designed and built for **Sunbeam English School, Bhagwanpur**, located in Bhagwanpur, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Highlights

- **Aesthetic & Brand Palette**:
  - **Academic Deep Blue** (`#063B66`): Primary brand color, headers, footers, and prominent visual cards.
  - **Warm Gold** (`#F8C531`): Admission CTAs, badge accents, and interactive highlights.
  - **Light Blue** (`#EAF4FB`) & **Neutral** (`#F5F7FA`): Soft background contrasts.
- **Typography**: Google Fonts — **Poppins** (Headings, titles, badges) and **Inter** (Readable body text, tables, forms).
- **Zero-Dependency Architecture**: Built with pure HTML5, vanilla CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript for blazing-fast load performance and offline reliability.
- **Responsive Layout**: Fluid grids and flexbox adapting seamlessly across desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Interactive Photo Gallery**: Category filter tabs (**Campus**, **Classrooms**, **Labs**, **Library**, **Sports**, **Activities**) with a full-screen lightbox modal supporting Next/Previous and keyboard arrows (`Escape`, `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`).
2. **Dedicated Science Laboratories**: Highlighting departmental experimentation environments for **Physics** (*The Newton*), **Chemistry** (*The Curie*), and **Biology** (*The Darwin*).
3. **Events & Celebrations**: Complete showcase for **Annual Function**, **Sports Day**, **Independence & Republic Day**, **Teachers' & Children's Day**, **Cultural Festivals**, and **Inter-School Competitions**.
4. **Admission Quick Modal**: One-click accessible modal enquiry form accessible from any page header or hero CTA button.
5. **Notice Board & Filter**: Filter notices by *All*, *Academic*, *Admissions*, and *Events* with full circular preview dialogs.
