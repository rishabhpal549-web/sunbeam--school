BEYOND ACADEMICS

School Activities

A diverse spectrum of co-curricular clubs enabling students to discover individual passions.

Cultural Activities

🎨

Art & Craft

Watercolors, sketching, pottery, origami, and annual student visual art exhibitions.

🎵

Music Club

Vocal Indian classical and light music, keyboard, tabla, and daily assembly choir performances.

💃

Extracurricular & Sports
